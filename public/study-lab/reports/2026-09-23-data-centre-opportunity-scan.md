# Data Centre Opportunity Scan

**Scan date:** 23 September 2026  
**Scope:** Global internships and co-ops, including relocation-heavy roles  
**Candidate lens:** BEng Cybersecurity Engineering at Concordia; technical product ownership, privacy/compliance, software delivery, analytics, and AI-enabled product work  
**Baseline:** Inaugural scan — there is no prior report to compare yet

## Executive readout

The market is thin for directly eligible data-centre internships today. One role is both live and highly actionable: Google's Canada Software Developer / Site Reliability internship. Apply by **25 September 2026**. It is not facilities work, but its SRE route is an unusually strong bridge into secure, resilient infrastructure.

The remaining live matches are global stretches. They fit either the cybersecurity/software profile or the data-centre/product-operations profile, but carry material timing, academic-year, or work-authorization constraints. They are useful signals for future scans, not equal-priority applications.

## Ranked opportunities

### 1. Google — Software Developer Intern, BS, Summer 2027

- **Location:** Waterloo or Toronto, Ontario
- **Fit:** 9/10
- **Action:** Apply now
- **Deadline:** 25 September 2026; applications are reviewed on a rolling basis and may close earlier
- **Why it fits:** The posting can route candidates into Site Reliability Engineering. That work includes hardening services against bad actors, troubleshooting traffic anomalies, monitoring and alerts, and building automation infrastructure. It connects cybersecurity engineering with production software and large-scale infrastructure while keeping the role in Canada.
- **Core requirements:** Bachelor's student in computer science or a related technical field; coding in at least two general-purpose languages; data structures or algorithms experience; full-time availability for 12 weeks; return to school afterward.
- **High-value signals to foreground:** Security and privacy product ownership; shipped software projects; Java plus JavaScript/TypeScript or Python; automation; technical communication; experience translating risk and regulation into product requirements.
- **Application package:** One-page technical resume, current transcript, graduation date, GitHub/portfolio links. Explicitly state interest in the SRE track.
- **Apply:** https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/123510626377966278-software-developer-intern-bs-summer-2027

### 2. Amazon — Software Development Engineer Intern, 2027

- **Location:** Dublin, Ireland
- **Fit:** 7.5/10
- **Action:** Explore immediately; verify immigration support and term alignment before investing heavily
- **Deadline:** Not stated; rolling while the posting remains open
- **Duration:** Three or six months
- **Why it fits:** The role focuses on distributed computing, storage, automation, scalability, and security. It also values communicating complex technical ideas to technical and business audiences — a strong match for a technical product and governance background.

### 3. Google — Security Engineering Intern, BS/MS, Summer 2027

- **Location:** Multiple United States offices
- **Fit:** 8.5/10 role fit; 3/10 current eligibility fit
- **Action:** Stretch only; confirm U.S. work authorization and academic-year eligibility
- **Deadline:** 9 October 2026; rolling review
- **Why it fits:** This is the strongest pure-cybersecurity role found. Work spans network hardening, attack monitoring, vulnerability discovery, offensive testing, and security automation. The role values privacy understanding and secure software development.
