02
Where this is going

The questions are already in hand.

The BEng is the foundation. My roadmap runs BEng → MSc → PhD, and the research territory has been visible from every layer of the descent: privacy engineering and usable security in regulated health systems — with the security of AI-agent systems as the forward edge.

01

Why do security controls die in real clinical workflows?

Controls that are correct on paper fail at the pharmacy counter. What does usable security look like when the user is overworked, the stakes are a patient’s medication, and the audit is months away?

02

Can regulation compile?

PIPEDA, PHIPA and Law 25 are read by lawyers and audited by checklists. What would it take to express them as verifiable properties of the schema itself — compliance as a build artifact, not an annual ritual?

03

Where is the security boundary when AI agents act for us?

Multi-agent systems now read, decide and write across applications. My own work (see Conductor, below) treats the broker as the entire trust boundary. That question deserves a research career, not a blog post.

I’ve spent ten years collecting the evidence. The next ten are for the answers.
